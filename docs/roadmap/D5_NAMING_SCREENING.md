# D5 — Naming screening (issue #35)

**Researched 17 November 2026** (about 14:40–15:20 UTC) on branch `docs/35-naming-screening`, from main `569b96f`. This is **preliminary screening from public sources, not trademark clearance, legal advice or a guarantee of availability.** No name has been applied: [#35](https://github.com/tylerbarnard23-PM/atlas-proposal-engine/issues/35) reserves the choice for the owner, and the product still ships as "Atlas Proposal Engine".

**Why rename:** the October 2026 check on #35 found close overlap with Telescope Atlas (AI-assisted portfolio construction and draft Statements of Advice), Meotis Finance Atlas (wealth-manager portfolio analysis and human-approved proposals) and Eton Solutions' AtlasFive. Those are the nearest neighbours of what this product is.

## Recommendation

**Cartouche — "Cartouche: Advisor Proposal Workspace."** Pronounced *kar-TOOSH*.

- **What it means.** On a map, the cartouche is the framed block that says what the sheet is: its title, scale, legend and sources. This product does exactly that job for a proposal: every value carries its source and date, the scope and its denominator are stated, the readiness checklist is visible and the document says what it is and is not. A framed, titled, documented page is also the product's output.
- **Continuity.** It keeps the cartography lineage of "Atlas", so the project's history and screenshots still read as the same thing, and "formerly Atlas Proposal Engine" is a natural note.
- **Claims.** It suggests no trading, no execution, no performance and no guaranteed outcome, and no capability the product lacks. The AI is a feature of the workspace, not part of the name, which keeps the "optional AI assistance" description honest.
- **Collisions.** Of the five names screened, it showed the fewest in wealth, advice and software: no advice firm, no software vendor and no software-class trademark in the checks below. Its exact name is used by an unrelated Swift game-launcher project on GitHub, which doesn't collide with this field.
- **Cost of choosing it.** The spelling and pronunciation are French, so some people will hesitate on them. Its single-word domains are all taken, though several practical alternatives were unregistered when checked (below).

**Runner-up: Theodolite** — a precision instrument for measuring angles, which suits the engine-checked figures, but it is longer, harder to spell, and a registered software-class trademark for a navigation and surveying app exists (below). If you prefer an unmistakable, less foreign word, choose it over the others.

## Screening by candidate

Each search is dated 17 November 2026. The web-search tool has limited coverage and a "nothing found" result is weak evidence; the trademark pages are third-party aggregators, not the USPTO's own database.

### Cartouche
- **Wealth and advice products:** searches of the name with fintech, wealth, investment and advisor software returned no vendor or adviser of this name; the closest hit was an unrelated film-production company profile. Seen results were advisor-tech roundups naming d1g1t, Quantfolio, QPLIX, BITA Wealth and fincite, none related.
- **Trademark aggregators:** records for CARTOUCHE exist in other classes (clothing, paper goods, auto parts, and Classes 3 and 14; some dead); **none in Classes 9, 36 or 42** in the sources seen, e.g. [CARTOUCHE 97107235](https://markinton.com/trademark/cartouche-97107235) and [CARTOUCHE 76659566](https://markinton.com/trademark/cartouche-76659566). Not a clearance.
- **GitHub:** exact-name repositories are unrelated — [cartouche-app/Cartouche](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche) (a Swift game launcher, 50★, active) and [Hakimba/cartouche](https://github.com/Hakimba/cartouche) (an Android game launcher).
- **Domains (registry RDAP and DNS, 17 November 2026):** `cartouche.com` (registered 1996), `.ai` (2025), `.app` (2026), `.dev` (2026) registered; `.io` has name servers. `getcartouche.com` and `usecartouche.com` registered; **`cartouchehq.com`, `cartouchedesk.com`, `cartoucheproposals.com`, `trycartouche.com` not found in the registry**. GitHub handles `cartouchehq`, `getcartouche`, `cartoucheai` free; `cartouche-app` taken.
- **Uncertain:** other jurisdictions, common-law use, social handles, and other meanings (an Egyptian name-ring; a French word for a cartridge).

## Method and limits

- **Name criteria:** distinctive; suits an advisor proposal workspace with optional AI; no suggestion of trading, execution, guaranteed performance or absent capabilities.
- **Web:** the session's web-search tool. Exact-name queries combined with fintech, wealth, investment, advisor, software and trademark terms. Coverage is limited and a miss proves little.
- **Trademark:** third-party aggregators (Justia, Markinton, GleanMark, TrademarkDir) as surfaced by search; some of these pages block automated fetching, so their details come from the search results and were not independently re-opened. **Not checked:** the USPTO's own TSDR and search system, state registers, the EUIPO, WIPO or the UK registry, common-law use, or any non-US class listing.
- **Domains:** the registries' own RDAP services (Verisign for `.com`, Identity Digital for `.ai`, Google Registry for `.app` and `.dev`) plus DNS. `.io` has no RDAP service in the IANA bootstrap, so only DNS was used and a name server is read as "registered". "Not found" means unregistered **at the moment checked**. It reserves nothing and can change at any time.
- **Handles:** GitHub user and organization names, observed through the public API. **Not checked:** X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Bluesky and the like, which can't be observed reliably without accounts. Treat those as uncertain.
- **Repository evidence:** GitHub's public search counts and top results on 17 November 2026; counts shift.
- Nothing was bought, registered or reserved. No settings or accounts were touched.

## Decision gate

**Naming is an explicit publication gate.** The project is not published or announced (LinkedIn or elsewhere) under a name the owner has not chosen after this screening. Before publication, the owner should also have counsel run a proper clearance on the chosen name, since this report doesn't make a legal-availability claim. After the owner's selection, the branding change follows [the implementation checklist](D5_BRANDING_IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.md) as one focused PR.
